Privacy

Privacy, without the fog.

Reading ByteBrief needs no account. If you sign in with Google, ByteBrief stores the small set of records needed to keep your reading list and topics in sync.

If you only read

No account is created, no profile is stored, and no reading history is sent anywhere. The pages fetch the public js/data.json file and the fonts they need. Your theme choice is kept in localStorage on your device.

If you sign in with Google

Google Authentication provides a display name, email address, photo URL, and a stable user ID. ByteBrief stores these in a Firestore document at users/{uid}, together with the topics you choose and a creation timestamp.

Saved stories are stored in the savedArticles collection. Each record contains your user ID, the story title, its link, the image URL supplied by the publisher, the source name, the category, and the time you saved it.

Where it lives

Authentication and Firestore are provided by Google Firebase. Data is stored in the Firebase project configured in js/firebase-config.js and is subject to Google Cloud's terms. ByteBrief has no separate application server and does not copy your account data anywhere else.

Update alerts

If you tap Turn on alerts, ByteBrief asks the browser for notification permission and stores a push subscription in Firestore. That subscription contains the browser's push endpoint and encryption keys. It does not contain your name, email, reading history, or topics. You can revoke permission in browser settings; ByteBrief cannot remove a browser-level denial.

  • bytebrief_pending_save briefly holds a story you tried to save before signing in. It is cleared as soon as the save finishes.
  • Older builds wrote bytebrief_login_time. The current build does not use it, and clearing site data removes it.

What ByteBrief does not collect

There are no analytics scripts, advertising networks, tracking pixels, session recordings, or third-party marketing cookies. ByteBrief does not build a reading history. The "For you" view is computed in your browser from the topics saved to your profile.

Third parties that still see a request

  • Google Fonts serves the Newsreader and Roboto typefaces.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and, like any host, may log request metadata.
  • Firebase handles sign-in and stored records.
  • Publishers receive your request when you open a story, view a hotlinked image, or visit an event or podcast link.

Those services have their own privacy policies. ByteBrief cannot control what a publisher does after you leave the site.

Your controls

You can sign out at any time from your profile. Removing a saved story deletes its Firestore document immediately. To delete your profile document and the remaining saved stories, open an issue on the ByteBrief repository from the account you want removed; there is no automated self-service deletion yet.

Firestore security rules restrict each account to its own profile and saved records. They are published in firestore.rules in the repository.

Children

ByteBrief is a general technology news site and is not directed at children under 13. It does not knowingly create profiles for children.

Changes

If the data model or service providers change, this page will be updated with the code change. The repository history is the authoritative record.
